Questions Parents Ask

What if a page is too hard?

Stop that page and step back, or show the same idea with objects or pictures. That temporary help is called scaffolding. Take it away when the work feels easier.

How much help should I give?

Show one example, try the next one together, then let your child try. That is scaffolding: help first, then fade the help. You do not need to give every answer.

When should we move forward?

Move on when this step feels comfortable with little help, not after one easy page. The IES early-math guide says a child should be comfortable with an earlier step before a harder one.

Why an organized pack instead of free worksheets?

A loose page can jump to a skill your child is not ready for. A pack keeps one progression, with a prompt, an answer key, and a tracker. You can still start with the free sample.
