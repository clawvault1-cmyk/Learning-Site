Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the right level?

Each product lists the skills your child should already know. If those feel easy, move up a level. The free sample is a good starting check.

What format are the worksheets?

PDF files sized for US Letter paper. They're designed for black-and-white home printing.

How do I get my files after purchase?

Checkout isn't live yet. Once it launches, you'll receive a download link right after payment.

How many pages are in each pack?

Page counts will be confirmed when each pack is finalized. They're listed as TBD for now.

Are the worksheets aligned to Common Core?

Our Kindergarten math content is informed by specific Common Core standards, which we list by code on each product page. We don't claim official certification or endorsement.

Can I share the files?

You may print them for your own children or your own classroom. Please don't redistribute or resell the files. See our Terms.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes, in certain situations. See our Refund Policy.

Do you collect information from children?

No. Our site is for adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
