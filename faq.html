Questions

Which pack should we start with?

Beginner: Adding within 5, if your child can count to 10. Move up when the pages feel easy.

I'm not a teacher. Can I do this?

Yes. The 2-page guide tells you what to say. Every page has an answer key.

How much should I help?

Show one problem, do the next one together, then let your child try. Help less as it gets easier.

What if a page is too hard?

Stop and count real things, like cereal, instead. Or go back a few pages. Try again tomorrow.

When should we move on?

When your child does most of a pack with little help. One easy page isn't enough to tell.

My child doesn't like worksheets. Will this help?

The pages are short, and you start with real things before the paper. Do one page and stop while it's going well.

Is any of this on a screen?

No. You print the pages, and your child uses a pencil.

What's in a pack?

18 practice pages, 18 answer keys, a 2-page parent guide, a cover, a progress tracker, and a certificate.

How do I get my files?

Right after you pay, you see a download page. You also get an email with the link. Check spam if you don't see it.

How does the weekly plan work?

It's $3.99/month. You get this month's packets right away, then a new one every Monday. Cancel anytime. When you cancel, access ends, so download the packets you want to keep.

How do I cancel?

Sign in to your account and cancel, or email us. See how to cancel.

Can I share the files?

You can print them for your family or one classroom. Please don't share or sell the files.

Can I get a refund?

If a file doesn't work or isn't what we described, we'll fix it. If we can't, we'll refund you. If you're new to the weekly plan and cancel within 7 days of your first charge, we'll refund that charge. See our refund policy.
