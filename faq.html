Questions

Which pack should we start with?

Beginner: Adding within 5, if your child can count 5 toys. Can't yet? Count apples or blocks up to 5 for a few days first.

I'm not a teacher. Can I do this?

Yes. Every page has an answer key, and the guide is 2 short pages in plain words.

What if a page is too hard?

Go back a page, or count real things like cereal. Try again tomorrow.

When do we move up?

When the last few practice pages are right with only a little help. The guide names the pages.

My child doesn't like worksheets. Will this help?

The pages are short, and you start with real things. Stop while it's still fun.

Is it on a screen?

No. You print it, and your child uses a pencil.

How do I get my files?

You get a download link right after you pay, and by email.

How does the weekly plan work?

$3.99/month. This month's packets today, then a new one every Monday. Cancel anytime. Access ends when you cancel.

Are weekly packets the same as the packs?

No. A weekly packet has 7 or 8 practice pages and a 1-page note. A pack goes deeper on adding, with the full 2-page guide, a tracker, and a certificate. You keep it.

How do I cancel?

Sign in and cancel, or email us.

Can I get a refund?

If a file has a problem, we'll fix it or refund you. New weekly members can get a full refund within 7 days.

Can I share the files?

Print them for your family, homeschool, or one classroom. Please don't share the files.
