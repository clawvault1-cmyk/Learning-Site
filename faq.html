Questions Parents Ask

What if a page is too hard?

Stop that page. Go back to an easier one, or show the same idea with counters. A hard page means the earlier step still needs practice.

How much help should I give?

Stay nearby. Let your child try. If they are stuck, show one example with objects, then hand the pencil back. You do not need to give every answer.

When should we move forward?

Move forward when the current pages feel comfortable and your child can tell you what they did. If you are helping with almost every problem, stay a little longer.

Why an organized pack instead of free worksheets?

Free pages are often mixed together, so the next page may jump in difficulty. A pack stays on one skill and shows the next step. You can still start with the free addition sample.

How do I choose a grade?

Use Choose a Grade on the homepage. Pick the grade, then Math or Reading, then a skill. Only Kindergarten Addition has packs today. Every other path says Coming Soon and links to the free sample.
