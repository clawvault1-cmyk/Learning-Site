We use it to:

  • handle refunds, stop fraud, and keep the site secure;

5. The Meta Pixel (ad measurement)

We may use the Meta Pixel, a small piece of code from Meta (the company behind Facebook and Instagram). We use it for ad measurement: to count how many people who saw our ads later visited, started a checkout, or bought, and to show our ads to people who might want them.

When it is on, the Pixel can send Meta things like: the page you viewed, the button you clicked, your IP address, browser details, and cookie IDs. Meta may match this to your Facebook or Instagram account. Payhip's checkout pages also use our Pixel ID, so Meta may learn that a purchase happened and its amount. If we send purchase details to Meta from our sales records, we send your email only in a scrambled ("hashed") form.

Meta uses this information under its own policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy

6. Your choices

  • Ads: You can change how Meta uses your activity for ads at https://accountscenter.facebook.com/ad_preferences, and you can turn off "activity from ad partners." You can also learn more and opt out of interest-based ads from many companies at https://optout.aboutads.info.
  • Cookies: You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site and checkout still work for buying.
  • Emails: Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • Your data: You can ask us what personal information we have about you, ask us to fix it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@proudlittlelearners.com. We will answer within 30 days. Some information, like sales records, we must keep for tax and legal reasons even if you ask us to delete it.

Some US states give their residents extra privacy rights. We will honor requests from any US resident as described above, whether or not your state's law applies to a business our size.

7. Children's privacy

Proud Little Learners sells printable worksheets to parents, guardians, and teachers. Our website and checkout are meant for adults 18 and older.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Children do not need to use our website to use the worksheets: an adult buys, downloads, and prints them. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, email hello@proudlittlelearners.com and we will delete it.

8. How long we keep information

  • Order and payment records: as long as tax and accounting rules require, usually up to 7 years. Payhip keeps its own transaction records under its policy.
  • Weekly plan consent and cancellation records: at least 3 years, or 1 year after your plan ends, whichever is longer, because subscription laws require it.
  • Support emails: up to 3 years, then deleted unless still needed for an open issue.
