  • Where to start. Each pack lists what your child should already be able to do, so the new work sits just past that.
  • The parent prompt. It tells you when to count together and when to let your child finish.
  • The page order. Groups you can count, then a picture, then a number sentence.
  • The tracker. You mark whether to repeat a page or move on.
