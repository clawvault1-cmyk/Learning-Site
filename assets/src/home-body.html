Level 2 · Intermediate

Within 10, with ten‑frames.

Ten-frames and a number line help your child see totals up to 10 and learn to count on from the bigger number.

Move up when adding within 5 with pictures feels easy.

Number bonds & making ten.

Your child breaks numbers apart, finds missing addends like 4 + __ = 7, works out the partner that makes ten, and solves simple word problems.

Our educational approach

Informed by frameworks educators know.

We are not affiliated with or endorsed by any of these organizations.
