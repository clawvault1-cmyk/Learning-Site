## 10 biggest changes
1. **The problem section is now just the ✗/✓ card.**
   - Old: 3 bullets + a "fix" paragraph + a 4-row card (101 words).
   - New: "Sound familiar?" + 3 ✗ (Where do I start? / Too hard, too fast / Ends in tears) vs 3 ✓ (Start at the right level / Small steps, in order / A guide tells you what to say). 33 words.
2. **"What 10 minutes looks like" → "10 minutes a day"**, with three short steps: Print one page. / Do it together. / Let your child try. 47 → 26 words.
3. **Pack cards drop to one line each.**
   - Old: "Count two small groups and put them together. Start here if your child can count to 10."
   - New: "For kids who can count to 10."
4. **Pack intro.** Old: "$3.99 each. Yours to keep. Not sure? Email us and we'll help you pick." → New: "$3.99 each. Yours to keep." The help line moves under the cards: "Not sure? Email us. We'll help you pick."
5. **Homepage weekly card.** Old: 2 lines + "Best if…" + 2 cancel lines. → New: "A new packet every Monday. Get this month's packets today." + $3.99/month + button. The terms live next to the real buy button on weekly.html.
6. **Homepage FAQ goes from 6 to 3 questions** (not a teacher / doesn't like worksheets / screen). 173 → 65 words.
7. **Closing section.**
   - Old: "Give your child the head start they deserve. Kindergarten math builds step by step. About 10 minutes a day now can make first grade easier."
   - New: "Give your child a strong start in math. Small steps, with help when they need it." It drops "deserve" and the first-grade promise.
8. **Product pages.**
   - Old: 3 paragraphs (practice / how you help / when to move on), plus a Common Core line.
   - New: "What your child does" + "What you say", one or two lines each, quoting the actual words a parent says. The Common Core line and "When to move on" are deleted.
9. **Weekly terms.**
   - Old: "Renews monthly until you cancel. Cancel online anytime. Access ends when you cancel. New members can get a full refund within 7 days."
   - New: "Renews monthly until you cancel. Cancel online anytime." / "Access ends when you cancel. New members: full refund within 7 days." All 4 required terms are kept, right under the button.
10. **FAQ page** goes from 13 long answers to 12 one-line answers. The refund answer goes from 46 words to 20 ("If a file has a problem, we'll fix it or refund you. New weekly members can get a full refund within 7 days."). About is cut to one line + email.
