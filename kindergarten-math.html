Kindergarten Math Worksheets

Start with addition, organized into three levels so your child practices exactly what they're ready for.

Kindergarten Addition

Level 1 · Beginner

Addition within 5

Uses pictures and objects. Your child counts two small groups and finds the total. Best for children who can count to 10 and recognize numbers 0–5.

Level 2 · Intermediate

Addition within 10

Moves from pictures to number sentences like 4 + 3 = __. Best for children who are comfortable adding within 5.

Level 3 · Advanced

Reasoning within 10

Simple word problems, breaking numbers apart, and finding the number that makes 10. Best for children who add within 10 confidently.
