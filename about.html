About Us

We make printable worksheets for parents, caregivers, homeschool families, and teachers of children in Pre-K through 2nd grade.

We started with one frustration: free worksheets online often jump between difficulty levels without warning. A child practicing 1 + 2 shouldn't suddenly face 17 − 6.

So we're building a library where every worksheet targets one skill at one clearly defined level, and each level leads to the next.

What we promise

  • Clear learning objectives and prerequisites on every product
  • Honest descriptions and previews
  • No inflated claims. Our work is informed by public frameworks, and we're not endorsed by them.

Where we are now

We're launching with Kindergarten Addition. Subtraction, reading, Pre-K, and 1st–2nd grade will follow, prioritized by what parents ask for. Tell us what you need.
