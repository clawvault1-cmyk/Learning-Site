About Us

We make printable worksheets for parents, caregivers, homeschool families, and teachers of children in Pre-K through 2nd grade.

Created for families across the United States, our printable learning packs make it easier to support early math and reading at home—with manageable steps and clear parent guidance.

You want to help. A pack tells you where to start, how to help, and what to practice next.

What you can count on

  • Each pack says what your child will practice, and what they should already be able to do.
  • Packs that are ready are marked Available now. Others are marked Coming soon.
  • A parent prompt, an answer key, and a progress tracker come with each pack.

Where we are now

Kindergarten addition levels are ready to buy one at a time. Proud Little Learners Weekly is $3.99 a month for a new packet every week. Other grades, subtraction, and reading are on the way. Tell us what you need.
