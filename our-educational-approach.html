How we teach

Three simple ideas many teachers use.

Pictures, then numbers

Your child counts pictures on the page, then writes number sentences, like 2 + 1 = 3. If counting is hard, count real apples or blocks first.

I do, we do, you do

On practice pages, you do problem 1 out loud. You do problem 2 together. Your child does the rest. The guide gives you one line to say, then you wait.

Easy to harder

The pages go from easy to harder. If a page is a guess, the guide names the page to go back to.

Where these ideas come from

  • Teaching Math to Young Children, a free guide from the U.S. Department of Education's research office (IES)
  • The Common Core kindergarten math standards
