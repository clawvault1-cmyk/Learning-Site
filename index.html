What 10 minutes tonight looks like

  1. Print one page

    Use the pack that matches what your child can already do.

  2. Try it together

    Let your child start. Stay close for about 10 minutes.

  3. Use the parent line

    If they pause, read the short prompt. Then let them finish, and mark the tracker.

Questions parents ask

Which pack should we start with?

Beginner: Adding within 5, if your child can count to 10 and read numbers 0–5. Move on when that pack feels easy.

What if a page feels too hard?

Stop and step back, or count real objects first. The parent prompt says when to help.

What do I get?

A 41-page PDF: 18 practice pages, 18 answer-key pages, a 2-page parent guide, a cover, a tracker, and a certificate.
