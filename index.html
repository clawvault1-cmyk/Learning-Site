Questions parents ask

I'm not a teacher. Can I do this?

Yes. The guide tells you what to say, and every page has an answer key.

Which pack should we start with?

Beginner: Adding within 5, if your child can count to 10.

My child doesn't like worksheets. Will this help?

The pages are short, and you start with real things like cereal before the paper. Do one page, stop while it's going well, and do the next one tomorrow.

How do I get it?

You get a download link right after you pay, plus an email with the same link. Print at home.

What's the difference between a pack and the weekly plan?

A pack is one level for $3.99, and you keep it. The weekly plan is $3.99/month for a new packet every Monday. Cancel anytime.

Are the weekly packets the same as the packs?

No. Weekly packets are shorter and cover more topics, like counting, shapes, and taking away. Each pack goes deeper on adding, with 18 pages.
