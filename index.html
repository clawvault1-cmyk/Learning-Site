Questions

Parent questions, answered simply.

What if a page is too hard?

Stop that page. Go back to an easier one, or show the same idea with counters on the table. A hard page means the step before it still needs practice. You do not have to push through.

How much help should I give?

Stay nearby. Read the directions if your child wants that. Let them try the first move. If they are stuck, show one example, then let them try the next problem. You do not need to supply every answer.

When should we move forward?

Move forward when the current pages feel comfortable and your child can tell you what they did. If you are helping with almost every problem, stay with this step a little longer.

Why use an organized pack instead of free worksheets?

Free pages can be useful. They are often mixed together, so the next page may jump to a much harder skill. A pack stays on one skill and tells you what comes next. A free addition sample is available if you want to try a page first.
