Questions parents ask

What will my child practice?

The full kindergarten addition path: count and combine groups up to 5, move to number sentences up to 10, then break numbers into parts, make 10, and solve short stories you can read aloud.

Is this a suitable starting point?

Choose the bundle if you want the next stage ready when this one feels easy, or if you are not sure yet which level fits. A child who already adds up to 5 with ease can start in the middle of the set. You can also begin with the free sample.

How does practice progress?

The packs stay in order. Nothing jumps from 1 + 2 to a much harder skill.

  1. Count groups
  2. Combine
  3. Pictures to equations
  4. Simple stories

What parent guidance is included?

Each level includes a short note on how to help. You do not need to finish a pack in one sitting. Use the note for the level you are on today.

When should we repeat a skill, and when should we move on?

Stay with a level while you are still helping with most problems. Move to the next level in the bundle when the current one feels comfortable and your child can tell you what they did.

What’s included?

All three packs: Beginner ($3.99), Intermediate ($3.99), and Advanced ($3.99). The bundle price is $9.99. Each pack is a black-and-white PDF for US Letter paper, with an answer key. Page counts will be added when the final files are ready.

The set is informed by Common Core kindergarten addition standards and Florida’s B.E.S.T. kindergarten math benchmarks. We are not endorsed by either organization. Read the Common Core kindergarten addition standards and Florida’s B.E.S.T. mathematics page.
