Questions parents ask

What will my child practice?

Combine small groups with totals up to 5, including a short addition story. Then add within 10 with pictures and number sentences. Then explore number relationships: parts of a number, making 10, and short stories you can read aloud.

Is this a suitable starting point?

Choose the bundle when you want the next stage ready. A child who already adds up to 5 with ease can start in the middle. You can also begin with the free sample.

How does practice progress?

The levels stay in this order.

  1. Count groups
  2. Combine
  3. Pictures to equations
  4. Simple stories

What parent guidance is included?

Each level includes a short prompt. You do not need to finish a level in one sitting. Use the prompt for the level you are on today.

When should we repeat a skill, and when should we move on?

Stay with a level while you are still helping with most problems. Move to the next level in the bundle when the current one feels comfortable and your child can tell you what they did. The progress tracker is there for that choice.

What’s included?

  • All three levels in one download
  • Where to start
  • Pages in order
  • A parent prompt
  • An answer key
  • A progress tracker
  • Black-and-white PDFs for US Letter paper
