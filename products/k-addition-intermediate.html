Questions parents ask

What will my child practice?

Your child moves from pictures to number sentences such as 4 + 3 = __. Some pages use a ten-frame, which is a box with 10 spaces, so numbers up to 10 are easier to see. Totals stay at 10 or below.

Is this a suitable starting point?

A fit when adding up to 5 with pictures feels easy, and your child recognizes numbers 0–10.

How does practice progress?

Pages go from a picture of the groups, to the number sentence, to counting on from the bigger number. A number line on some pages shows that count.

The full kindergarten path is:

  1. Count groups
  2. Combine
  3. Pictures to equations
  4. Simple stories

What parent guidance is included?

Point to the bigger number and count the rest together one time. Then let your child try the next problem. If the ten-frame is new, fill it with counters before using the pencil.

When should we repeat a skill, and when should we move on?

Stay here if the number sentences still feel like a guess. Move to the Advanced pack when adding up to 10 feels comfortable.
