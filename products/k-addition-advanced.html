Questions parents ask

What will my child practice?

Your child breaks a number into two parts, such as 7 as 4 and 3. They find a missing number, like 4 + __ = 7, and find the number that makes 10. Short stories ask what happens when groups are joined. You can read the stories out loud. Your child does not have to read them alone.

Is this a suitable starting point?

A fit when your child adds up to 10 with confidence. Plan to read the little stories yourself.

How does practice progress?

This is the last step in the kindergarten addition path we have now: parts of a number, then a missing part, then a simple story. Subtraction packs are not ready yet.

The full kindergarten path is:

  1. Count groups
  2. Combine
  3. Pictures to equations
  4. Simple stories

What parent guidance is included?

Use objects to act out the story. Ask what is happening before you ask for the total. If a missing number is sticky, go back to two groups your child can see.

When should we repeat a skill, and when should we move on?

Stay with this pack if missing numbers still need a lot of help. When the pages feel comfortable, your child has finished the kindergarten addition levels available now. Subtraction is coming soon.

Learning goals

These pages practice goals from Common Core K.OA.A.2 (stories within 10), K.OA.A.3 (breaking numbers up to 10 into pairs), and K.OA.A.4 (finding the number that makes 10). You can read the stories aloud. Common Core kindergarten addition.
