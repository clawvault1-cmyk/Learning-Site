Questions parents ask

What will my child practice?

Your child counts two small groups, puts them together, and writes the total. A page might show 2 dots and 1 more dot, then the number sentence 2 + 1 = 3. The pack also includes a short addition story you can read aloud. Totals stay at 5 or below.

Is this a suitable starting point?

This is a good starting point if your child can count to 10, recognize the numbers 0–5, and count objects one by one.

How does practice progress?

The pages move from counting each group, to combining the groups, to a number sentence, and then to a short addition story. Totals stay at 5 or below.

The full kindergarten path is:

  1. Count groups
  2. Combine
  3. Pictures to equations
  4. Simple stories

What parent guidance is included?

Stay nearby. Let your child count out loud. A prompt you can use: “Point to the two groups. How many in all?” If they pause, count one group together, then let them count the rest.

When should we repeat a skill, and when should we move on?

Stay with this pack if you are helping with most of the problems. Move to the Intermediate pack when adding up to 5, including the short story, feels easy.
