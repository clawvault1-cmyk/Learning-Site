Weekly plan

Proud Little Learners Weekly is $3.99 a month. The plan is a new printable math or reading packet each week, for a grade you pick, from Preschool through 2nd grade.

Billing. $3.99 is charged once each month. Billing starts only when checkout is open. Until then, no payment is taken.

Auto-renew. The plan renews automatically each month until you cancel. We will not start a new charge after you cancel.

How to cancel. You can cancel anytime online in a couple of clicks. When billing is open, the email that comes with each charge will include a cancel link. There is no app, and there is no separate account page.

After you cancel. The next month is not charged. Packets you already downloaded stay yours to print for your own children, homeschool, or single classroom, under the license above. New packets stop. Canceling does not by itself refund a month you already paid. See the Refund Policy.
