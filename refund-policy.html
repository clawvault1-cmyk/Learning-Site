Weekly plan

Proud Little Learners Weekly is $3.99 a month and renews automatically until you cancel. You can cancel anytime online in a couple of clicks. Canceling stops the next monthly charge. It does not refund a month that was already paid, unless a packet from that month is broken, will not download, or does not match its description. Contact us within 14 days of that packet and we will fix it or refund that charge.

Packets you already downloaded stay yours to print under the license in the Terms. New packets stop after you cancel. There is no app.
